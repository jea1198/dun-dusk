Studio Policy

A few simple guidelines help us give every client the time and care their appointment deserves. Please take a moment to read them before booking.

  1. 01

    Guarantee

    Shellac, BIAB and acrylic treatments are guaranteed for 72 hours. If a nail lifts, chips or breaks within that time because of our work, we will repair it free of charge. Just get in touch as soon as you notice.

    Regular polish is not covered. Once you leave the studio, we cannot be held responsible for damage or infection, so please follow the aftercare advice given at your appointment.

  2. 02

    Cancellations

    Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule, so the time can be offered to another client.

  3. 03

    Missed Appointments

    After three missed appointments without notice, future visits will be walk-in only, or a non-refundable deposit will be required to secure a booking.

  4. 04

    Late Arrival

    If you are running late, please call or message us. Arriving more than 10 minutes after your booked time may mean we are unable to complete your full treatment.

  5. 05

    Refunds

    We do not offer refunds once a treatment has been paid for. If something isn't right, let us know within the guarantee period and we will do our best to put it right.

  6. 06

    Children

    Children under 12 must be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times. Parents and guardians are responsible for any damage caused in the studio.

  7. 07

    Gift Vouchers

    Gift vouchers are non-refundable, non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash. Please bring your voucher with you to your appointment.

  8. 08

    Health & Allergies

    Please tell us about any medical conditions, allergies or sensitivities before your treatment begins, so we can adjust products and technique where needed.

  9. 09

    Right to Refuse

    For the safety of our clients and team, we may decline a treatment where there are open wounds or a contagious skin or nail condition. We also reserve the right to refuse service in the case of inappropriate behaviour towards staff.

  10. 10

    Personal Belongings

    Please keep your belongings with you. We cannot accept responsibility for items lost or damaged in the studio.

These policies help us give every client the time and care they deserve. By booking with Dún & Dusk, you agree to the policies above. Thank you for understanding.
